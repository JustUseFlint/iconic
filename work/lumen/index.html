Case study 06Lumen Health

The Night Shift

A sleep app rebuilt around the 22 million Americans who work while everyone else sleeps.

Client
Lumen Health
Year
2023
Sector
Health & Wellbeing
Services
Strategy, Campaign, Product
Markets
US
Duration
12 months

01 — The brief

Lumen was one of 400 sleep apps in the App Store, competing on soothing sounds and celebrity voices. Downloads were steady but 70% of users churned within a month. The board wanted a reason to exist, and a path to partnerships with employers.

Awake.

02 — The insight

Every sleep app was designed for people who sleep at night.

One in five American workers works shifts. Nurses, drivers and warehouse teams were downloading sleep apps and finding advice that assumed a 10pm bedtime. Lumen could become the first sleep brand built for the night shift.

Lumen HealthThe Night ShiftInstrument Serif — Strategy / Campaign / Product
01 / 03

03 — The work

01

The repositioning

From “sleep better” to “sleep whenever you can”: a brand for people whose day starts at 7pm.

02

The product

Rota-aware sleep plans that sync with shift calendars, rebuilt with 140 shift workers in 9 weeks of testing.

03

The campaign

Ads that ran only between midnight and 6am, in hospital break rooms, on overnight radio and on late-night feeds.
