Case study 02Casa Fresca

Cooked fresh, ready Tuesday.

A custom Shopify theme for Casa Fresca, Sergio Landaverde’s weekly meal-prep and catering kitchen in Crawfordsville, Indiana. This week’s menu comes first, and every meal is one tap from the cart.

01 — The brief

Casa Fresca cooks a new menu every week. Customers order by Thursday at 6 PM, the kitchen cooks on Monday and Tuesday, and meals are picked up from the cooler at Ironsmith’s Gym in Crawfordsville or delivered on Tuesday. The kitchen also caters company dinners, work lunches, rehearsal dinners and weddings across central Indiana. The job was a Shopify theme that makes the weekly order quick on a phone and keeps the cut-off impossible to miss.

Tuesday.

02 — The insight

Most visits have one job: get this week’s meals in before Thursday at 6 PM.

So the home page opens on this week’s menu, not a banner. The cut-off and the ready day sit beside the headline with a live countdown, sides and bases are picked right on the meal card, and the cart is one tap away on every page.

01 / 03

03 — The work

01

The menu

This week’s meals lead the home page. Each card shows protein, calories and carbs, lets you choose a base such as white rice, brown rice or quinoa, and adds to the cart in one tap.

02

The week

An announcement bar and a date card count down to the Thursday 6 PM cut-off and show the Tuesday ready day. How it works lays the week out in four steps, with pickup at Ironsmith’s Gym and Tuesday delivery side by side.

03

The extras

Catering has its own page with recent events and an inquiry form. A macro calculator and a nutrition quiz help regulars choose, and phones get a floating cart with the item count and total.
