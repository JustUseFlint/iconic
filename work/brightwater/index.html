Case study 08Brightwater FC

Ours Since 1891

Giving a 133-year-old football club its identity back after promotion to the top flight.

Client
Brightwater FC
Year
2026
Sector
Sport
Services
Identity, Social, Campaign
Markets
UK & global fans
Duration
8 months

01 — The brief

Promotion brought Brightwater FC global broadcast exposure and a crest that had been simplified into a generic shield years earlier. Fans hated it. The club needed an identity ready for a worldwide audience that its oldest supporters would recognise as theirs.

Ours.

02 — The insight

The fans were the brand guardians. We just had to listen.

We ran 18 fan forums and opened the club archive to supporters. The answer was in a 1920s programme: a hand-cut crest of the old ferry and the river, lost for a century.

03 — The work

01

The crest

The 1920s ferry crest, redrawn for screens as small as 16 pixels and kits as large as a stand.

02

The type

A condensed typeface drawn from the club’s original painted stadium signage, now used everywhere from tickets to broadcast.

03

The launch

The crest was revealed by fans, not the club: 12,000 supporters held up cards in the home end on the first day of the season.
