Case study 02Norrow

Money, made plain

Launching a digital bank in the most crowded category in Europe by saying less than everyone else.

Client
Norrow
Year
2024
Sector
Financial Services
Services
Naming, Identity, Launch, Product
Markets
UK & Ireland
Duration
11 months

01 — The brief

A team of former central bankers had a licence, a product and no name. Eleven digital banks had launched in the UK in five years, all promising to be friendly, simple and different. Norrow needed one million accounts in its first year to hit its funding milestone, against competitors spending four times as much.

Plain.

02 — The insight

Everyone was promising simple. Nobody was being simple.

We reviewed 2,400 pieces of fintech advertising and found an average of 38 words per ad. The more a bank claimed to be simple, the more it explained. So we built an entire brand on the discipline of saying one thing at a time.

03 — The work

01

The name

Norrow, from the old Norse for “narrow strait”: a straight passage through rough water. Screened in 14 languages, with a domain bought for £18,000.

02

The system

One colour, one typeface, one sentence per screen. Every ad and every app screen obeys the same rule: one idea, never more.

03

The launch

Cobalt panels with a single line took over 1,100 Underground sites. The app hit number one in UK finance on day three.
