Hunt. Search. Find.

01 — The brief

Aerial Hunter flies a DJI Matrice 4T thermal drone for deer recovery, lost-pet searches, herd counts and land checks. Home base is Rockville, in Parke County, and the service covers the whole state. Its story lived on a Facebook page. It needed a real website that explains the service in seconds and puts the phone number one tap away, because nobody looking for a wounded deer or a missing dog wants to read for long.

Find.

02 — The insight

People reach for this service in a hurry. The site has to answer fast and get them on the phone.

Someone who has just lost a blood trail or a dog is standing in a field with a phone. So the site leads with the number, explains thermal search in one picture instead of a paragraph, and makes it plain that the drone can come to them anywhere in Indiana.

01 / 03

03 — The work

01

The scan

The home page opens on a thermal view you can sweep with your cursor or finger, with a lock-on box that settles on a heat signature. It shows what the drone does before a word is read.

02

The tools

A palette switcher shows one scene four ways (naked eye, white hot, black hot and ironbow), a statewide service-area map shows home base and travel, and a conditions check helps callers know when a flight makes sense.

03

The call

Tap-to-call and text buttons stay one thumb away on every page, with service pages for deer recovery, lost pets and herd and land surveying, plus how it works, the service area, about and FAQ.
