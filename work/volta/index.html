Ride the City

Turning an e-bike subscription into the fastest way across six of the world’s most congested cities.

Client
Volta
Year
2026
Sector
Mobility
Services
Campaign, Out-of-home, Media
Markets
6 cities
Duration
7 months

01 — The brief

Volta had a beautiful bike and a $29 monthly subscription but a perception problem: e-bikes were seen as toys for weekends. The goal was 60,000 subscribers across six launch cities within a year, mostly converting car and transit commuters.

Faster.

02 — The insight

Commuters do not want a lifestyle. They want their 40 minutes back.

We timed 1,200 real commutes across six cities. On average, Volta beat the car by 17 minutes and the bus by 22. Rather than sell freedom or fun, we sold the clock.

03 — The work

01

The race

We raced Volta riders against cars, buses and taxis at rush hour, live-streamed, every Monday for 10 weeks. Volta won 54 of 60 races.

02

The clock

Dynamic billboards showed live journey-time comparisons pulled from city traffic data, updated every five minutes.

03

The offer

A free first week, delivered to the door in under 48 hours, removed the last reason to wait.
