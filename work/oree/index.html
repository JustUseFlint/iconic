Case study 06Maison Orée

Light, Bottled

The debut fragrance from a Parisian jeweller, launched with a film made entirely with natural light.

Year
2026

01 — The brief

Maison Orée, a 120-year-old Parisian jeweller, was entering fragrance for the first time. The category spends more on launch than almost any other, and most launches look the same: celebrity, slow motion, gold. Orée wanted a launch that felt as considered as its jewellery, and a fragrance that sold out its first production run.

Golden.

02 — The insight

A jeweller’s craft is the management of light.

Orée’s workshop on Rue de la Paix still sets stones by daylight only. We built the whole launch around that discipline: no artificial light in any frame, any photograph or any installation.

03 — The work

01

The film

Shot over 31 golden hours in Provence and Paris, using only the sun and reflectors. No lighting rig ever arrived on set.

02

The bottle

A faceted flacon cut like a cushion diamond, designed with the Orée workshop to throw caustic light across a table.

03

The salon

A daylight-only pop-up in Le Marais, open from sunrise to sunset, with timed entry. 41,000 visitors in six weeks.
