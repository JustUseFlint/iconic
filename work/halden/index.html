Case study 02Halden Outdoor

Into the Quiet

A 90-year-old outdoor brand stopped shouting about summits and started selling silence.

Client
Halden Outdoor
Year
2026
Sector
Retail & Apparel
Services
Brand platform, Campaign, Film
Markets
11 markets
Duration
10 months

01 — The brief

Halden had spent a decade chasing extreme-sports imagery and losing ground to louder, younger brands. Sales were flat, the average customer was 54, and nobody under 35 could tell us what the brand stood for. The brief was simple to write and hard to answer: make Halden matter to a new generation without abandoning the people who had trusted it for three.

Quiet.

02 — The insight

People were not going outside to conquer anything. They were going to get away from the noise.

Across 62 interviews in Oslo, Denver and Manchester, the word that came up most was not adventure or challenge. It was quiet. The outdoors had become the last place people could think. Halden had made gear for that for 90 years. It just had never said so.

03 — The work

01

The platform

“Into the Quiet” reframed Halden from performance gear to permission: a brand for the hours you spend away from everything else.

02

The film

A four-minute film with almost no dialogue, shot over 19 days across Norway and the Scottish Highlands, cut into 140 edits for every platform.

03

The retail

Flagships were rebuilt around a sound-dampened “quiet room” where customers could try gear in near silence. Dwell time tripled.
