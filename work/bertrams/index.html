Case study 04Bertram’s Elite Mobile Detailing

Showroom shine, delivered.

A website for Bertram’s Elite Mobile Detailing, Corbin Bertram’s veteran-owned detailing business in Trafalgar, Indiana. Eight services, a gallery of real jobs and a three-step quote, with the phone number always in reach.

01 — The brief

Bertram’s is owned and run by Corbin Bertram out of Trafalgar, Indiana. It is veteran owned, certified on the ceramic coatings it installs and built on word of mouth. Bertram’s does ceramic coating, paint protection film, window tint, paint correction, interior and exterior detailing, headlight restoration and boat detailing, and it goes to the customer anywhere in central Indiana. The site had to show the standard of the work and make it easy to ask for a price.

At a glance

Bertram’s Elite Mobile Detailing, on desktop and phone.

www.bertramelitemobiledetailing.com ↗

02 — The insight

People who care about their paint want proof first and a price second.

So the home page leads with a real truck in a real driveway, and the work shows up early and often: a services grid made from job photos, a filterable gallery and boats on their own page. Getting a price is a short three-step quote, and call or text sits in the header on every page.

01 / 03

03 — The work

01

The services

Eight services in three groups: protection, correction and care, and marine. Each has its own page with what you get, how it is done and what pairs well with it, and the home page shows them as a grid built from photos of the work.

02

The work

A gallery of recent jobs, from a Ram 2500 Longhorn interior to a coated hood and a runabout at the dock, filters by protection, correction and care, and marine, so a visitor finds a job like theirs.

03

The quote

Three quick steps cover the vehicle, the service and how to reach you, with the phone number beside the form for anyone who would rather talk. The home page also maps the towns Bertram’s drives to across central Indiana.
