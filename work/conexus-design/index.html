Case study 03Conexus Design

Spaces that connect, serve and uplift.

A portfolio website for Conexus Design, the architecture practice of Adam N. Chester, AIA, NCARB. Twenty church, medical and commercial projects, six services and nine licensed states, presented as calmly as the buildings themselves.

01 — The brief

Conexus Design is an architectural firm led by founder Adam N. Chester, AIA, NCARB. It offers contract design, virtual reality walk-throughs, 3D illustrations, planning, programming, schematic and detail design, construction documents and construction administration, mostly for churches, medical clinics and commercial buildings. The practice is licensed in nine states and works for builders and congregations far from home. It needed a site that lets a building committee or a contractor see the body of work quickly and trust the person behind it.

At a glance

Conexus Design, on desktop and phone.

www.conexusdesign.com ↗

02 — The insight

Architecture clients buy the portfolio and the person. The site should get out of the way of both.

So the site is quiet and dark, with big images and light type. The home page is one full-bleed image and one line. The projects page leads with the count, a map of where the work is and filters for medical, religious and commercial. The about page puts Adam, his licences and what clients say about him side by side.

01 / 03

03 — The work

01

The first look

The home page opens on a single full-bleed architectural image with the line “Spaces that connect, serve, and uplift.” A Menu pill opens a compact panel where each link shows a preview image of where it leads, and the logo mark draws itself in.

02

The portfolio

The projects page counts up to twenty, shows where the work is on a map of the United States, and filters by medical, religious and commercial. Every project has its own page with location, type and state, and steps forward and back through all twenty.

03

The practice

Six service pages explain each phase in plain words, from concept sketches to construction administration and 3D capture. The about page introduces Adam, lists the nine states he is licensed in and shares what builders and homeowners say about working with him.
