Case study 04Kettle & Crane

The Slow Pour

A family tea company with world-class leaves and invisible packaging became the brand people photograph.

01 — The brief

Kettle & Crane sourced some of the best single-estate tea in North America but sold it in brown pouches that disappeared on shelf. A national grocery listing was on the table if the brand could prove it would move off the shelf. We had nine months and a packaging budget smaller than most launch parties.

Steep.

02 — The insight

Tea is not a drink people rush. The brand should not rush either.

Shelf research showed shoppers spent an average of 4.2 seconds in the tea aisle. Rather than shout louder, we designed packaging that rewarded a second look: hand-drawn cranes that change with every blend, and tins worth keeping.

03 — The work

01

The tins

Reusable tins with a single continuous-line crane for each of 14 blends. Production cost came in 11% under the old pouches.

02

The ritual

A brewing guide printed inside every lid turned an unboxing into a five-minute ritual worth sharing.

03

The store

A rebuilt Shopify store with a tea-finder quiz lifted conversion and tripled the subscription base.
