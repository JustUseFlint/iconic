Case study 08Corvid Audio

Hear the Room

Launching a challenger headphone with spatial audio by letting people hear the rooms where music is made.

Client
Corvid Audio
Year
2023
Sector
Consumer Tech
Services
Launch, Social, Media
Markets
US, UK, Japan
Duration
6 months

01 — The brief

Corvid, a 40-person audio company from Portland, was launching its first flagship headphone against category giants with 50× its marketing budget. Its spatial audio was genuinely better, but “better spatial audio” is impossible to demonstrate in a six-second ad.

Listen.

02 — The insight

You cannot describe a sound. You can only put people in the room.

Musicians told us the room shapes a record as much as the instrument. Corvid’s audio could recreate those rooms. So we recorded twelve artists in twelve legendary rooms and let listeners step inside.

03 — The work

01

The sessions

Twelve artists, twelve rooms, from a Tokyo jazz kissa to a church in Reykjavík, recorded in full spatial audio.

02

The creators

600 audio creators received early units and the sessions, generating 14,000 reaction videos in the first month.

03

The media

A media plan bought almost entirely in audio and creator environments, where the product could be heard, not just seen.
