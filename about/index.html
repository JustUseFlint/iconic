Independentsince 2004

(Who we are)

Iconic started in a two-room studio on the Bowery with four people and one client. Twenty-two years later we are 640 people in five cities, still independent, and since 2020 owned by the people who work here. We have never taken outside investment, never pitched for free and never stopped believing that the best marketing is the kind people choose to pay attention to.

What we believe

  1. 01

    Say one thing.

    The brands people remember are the ones that edited hardest.

  2. 02

    Earn attention.

    If people would skip it, we have not finished yet.

  3. 03

    Measure honestly.

    We agree objectives with finance, not just marketing, and publish how we did.

  4. 04

    Stay small inside.

    No team larger than nine. No idea without an owner.

History

Twenty-two years, one address.

2004
  1. 2004

    Margot Ellery opens Iconic with four people in a two-room studio on the Bowery.

  2. 2006

    First national campaign, for Pinegate Air. The team grows to 25.

  3. 2009

    London studio opens on Paradise Row with eight people.

  4. 2011

    First Cannes Lions Grand Prix, for Northfield Energy.

  5. 2014

    Performance Media practice launches with a team of 12 analysts.

  6. 2016

    Lisbon studio opens, home to the design and type studio.

  7. 2018

    Singapore studio opens to serve clients across Asia-Pacific.

  8. 2020

    Iconic becomes employee-owned through an Employee Ownership Trust.

  9. 2021

    Los Angeles studio opens, focused on film and production.

  10. 2023

    Named Independent Agency of the Year for the third time.

  11. 2026

    640 people across five studios. Still independent, and still on the Bowery.
