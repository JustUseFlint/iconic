Iconic appoints Arjun Mehta as Global Head of Performance Media

Mehta joins from a leading retail media network to scale the agency’s outcome-based media model.

Iconic Communications11 Mar 20253 min read

For immediate release

LONDON — 11 MAR 2025 — Iconic has appointed Arjun Mehta as Global Head of Performance Media. Mehta joins from a leading retail media network, where he led measurement and partnerships for nine years.

He will lead Iconic’s 120-person media practice across New York, London and Singapore, with a remit to scale the agency’s outcome-based fee model and its incrementality testing platform.

The best media plans start with the work. Iconic is one of the few places where analysts and creatives sit at the same table.— Arjun Mehta

Mehta reports to Chief Operating Officer Kenji Arakawa and is based in the London studio.

About Iconic

Iconic is an independent brand and marketing company founded in 2004. Employee-owned since 2020, it has 640 people in studios in New York, London, Lisbon, Singapore and Los Angeles, and works with clients including Halden Outdoor, Norrow, Maison Orée and Brightwater FC.
