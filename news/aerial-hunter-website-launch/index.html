Aerial Hunter’s new website is live

A ten-page site for a Parke County thermal drone pilot, built to get a caller from search to phone in seconds.

Flint Newsroom9 Nov 20262 min read

Aerial Hunter, a thermal drone search and recovery service based in Parke County, Indiana, now has a website built by Flint at justuseflint.github.io/aerial-hunter.

Aerial Hunter is run by an FAA Part 107 certified pilot who flies a DJI Matrice 4T for deer recovery, lost-pet searches, herd counts and land checks across Parke County and the counties around it.

Someone who has just lost a blood trail or a dog is standing in a field with a phone. Everything on the site works back from that moment.

The ten-page site opens on a thermal view you can sweep with a cursor or a finger, shows one scene in four thermal palettes, maps the counties served and keeps tap-to-call and text one thumb away on every page.
