Casa Fresca’s new Shopify theme is live

A custom theme for a weekly meal-prep and catering kitchen in Crawfordsville, built so this week’s menu comes first and the Thursday cut-off is impossible to miss.

Flint Newsroom10 Nov 20262 min read

Casa Fresca, Sergio Landaverde’s weekly meal-prep and catering kitchen in Crawfordsville, Indiana, now runs on a custom Shopify theme designed and built by Flint. It is live at casafrescameals.com.

Casa Fresca cooks a new menu every week. Customers order by Thursday at 6 PM, the kitchen cooks on Monday and Tuesday, and meals are picked up from the cooler at Ironsmith’s Gym in Crawfordsville or delivered on Tuesday. The kitchen also caters company dinners, work lunches, rehearsal dinners and weddings across central Indiana.

Most visits have one job: get this week’s meals in before Thursday at 6 PM. The theme is built around that.

The home page opens on this week’s menu rather than a banner. Each meal card shows protein, calories and carbs, lets you pick a base such as white rice, brown rice or quinoa, and adds to the cart in one tap. A countdown keeps the Thursday cut-off and the Tuesday ready day in view, and phones get a floating cart with the item count and total.

Catering has its own page with recent events and an inquiry form, and a macro calculator and a nutrition quiz help regulars choose. The theme has 38 custom sections, carries over more than 80 meals from the store, uses two self-hosted typefaces and respects reduced-motion settings.
