Norrow’s “Money, made plain” wins the Grand Effie

The launch campaign that opened 1.4 million accounts takes the top honour for marketing effectiveness.

Iconic Communications19 Jun 20263 min read

Norrow’s launch campaign “Money, made plain” has won the Grand Effie at this year’s UK Effie Awards, the highest honour for marketing effectiveness.

The campaign helped the digital bank open 1.4 million accounts in nine months, four months ahead of plan, while reducing the cost per funded account by 41% against its business case.

The judges were struck by the discipline of the idea. One sentence per screen, one idea per ad, and a business result that speaks for itself.— Chair of the Effie UK jury

The win is Iconic’s sixth Grand Effie and its second for a financial services brand.
